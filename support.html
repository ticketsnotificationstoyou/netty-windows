Frequently Asked Questions

Netty won't start / shows an error about sing-box
Make sure you installed Netty using the official installer. If the problem persists, try reinstalling.
The app shows "VPN started but internet is unavailable"
This means your VPN server is unreachable or misconfigured. Check your server link and try again.
How do I add a server?
Click the + button and choose one of three methods — paste from clipboard, enter manually, or scan a QR code.
What protocols are supported?
VLESS, VLESS Reality, VMess, Trojan, and Shadowsocks.
What is Traffic Routing?
Routing lets you control which traffic goes through the VPN and which goes directly. You can:
  • Upload a .txt file with IP/CIDR ranges to exclude or route through VPN
  • Add domains (one per line) and set them to go Direct, Via VPN, or Block

When routing is active, the routing button glows orange and an orange icon appears on the main green indicator.

Where is my data stored?
All data (servers, routing rules, settings) is stored locally on your device at %AppData%\Netty\. Nothing is sent to our servers.
How do I change the app language?
The language is selected during installation. To change it, reinstall and choose a different language.
How do I uninstall?
Use Windows Settings → Apps, find Netty and uninstall. You will be asked whether to delete your saved servers and settings.
