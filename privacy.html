Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026
🔒
We collect no data whatsoever.

Netty does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, usage statistics, analytics, crash reports, or any other data.

Overview

Netty is a VPN client for Windows developed by Hash and Joy, LLC. We are committed to your privacy.

What Stays on Your Device

All of the following is stored exclusively on your device and never leaves it:

  • VPN server configurations and connection links you add
  • Routing rules (IP/CIDR lists and domain lists)
  • App settings and preferences

VPN Servers

Netty is a client tool only. We do not provide, operate, or have any access to VPN servers. You connect to servers that you configure yourself. We have no knowledge of which servers you use or what traffic passes through them.

Network Access

Netty makes network connections solely to:

  • The VPN server you have configured and chosen to connect to
  • connectivitycheck.gstatic.com — to verify that your connection is working after connecting

No other outbound connections are made by the app.

Third-Party Components

Netty uses sing-box as its core tunneling engine. sing-box is open source software. Its own privacy practices apply to its operation.

Children

Netty does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
